## Customer
Balcony / patio container gardeners and houseplant owners who travel 1–3 weeks (summer holidays, work trips) and have no reliable plant-sitter; no outdoor tap or power on many apartment balconies. [INFERENCE from S-B24, S-B27, S-B30]

## Problem
- Pots dry out faster than beds; keeping containers alive while away is a recurring seasonal question answered by US university extension services (UF/IFAS, K-State, WSU, Missouri, Colorado State). [FACT — existence of these pages, S-B26, S-B27, S-B30]
- UF/IFAS lists self-watering containers, pebble trays, wicks and drip emitters on timers as options. [FACT per search summary, S-B27]
- Existing low-tech fixes have limits: watering bulbs ~7 days; terracotta spikes / ollas ~7–21 days depending on size. [FACT per search summary of a blog, S-B24 — weak]
- Gravity drip pain points: low pressure gives uneven output (first emitter waters more than the last); "significant fiddling" to get flow right — too slow wets only part of the soil, too fast drains the reservoir through the pots; clogging of small drippers; success often requires raising the tank. [FACT per search summaries, S-B26 (Colorado State Extension / Drip Depot support), S-B29 — the latter weak]
- Frequency: UNKNOWN; seasonal (INFERENCE).

## Product
Kit: 5–10 L collapsible reservoir bag with hanging strap and level marks, manifold + 6–12 adjustable **micro in-line valves** (larger orifice, less clogging per S-B26) rather than tiny drippers, 1/4" tubing, pot stakes, inline mesh filter, and a printed calibration card ("drops per minute → mL/day") with a 48-hour dry-run procedure before leaving. No pump, no battery, no electronics.
Good vs bad: valve precision and stability, leak-free fittings, UV-stable tubing, bag seam strength.

## Shipping concerns
Light, soft, no liquids shipped, no batteries. [INFERENCE]

## Return / breakage concerns
Breakage low (flexible parts). Main risk is **functional failure**: uneven flow, clogging, or a leak that floods a balcony/indoor floor → refund and reputational damage (S-B26). Mitigation: dry-run procedure, drip tray recommendation, valve QC. [INFERENCE]
