## Customer
Adult diamond-painting (diamond art / diamond dot) hobbyists who keep leftover drills between kits and work on several canvases, sometimes away from home. [INFERENCE from product positioning S-C21, S-C22; "leftover materials" positioning in S-C20/S-C22 snippets]

## Problem
- Tiny resin drills (one container ≈10 g / ~2,000 drills per ARTDOT) spill when lids pop open; buyers report some lids in multi-container sets didn't shut and popped open when pressed; a critical review worried lids/tabs aren't tight enough to stay closed if a container falls out. [FACT per search snippets of S-C19 — Amazon review page not opened]
- Pouring drills into small jars spills; reviewers suggest pouring over a tray. [FACT per S-C19 snippet]
- A 2026 review site states screw-top containers held when shaken upside down. [FACT per S-C20 — weak source]
- Workarounds: bead organizers, pill boxes, zip bags, screw-top jars. [INFERENCE]
- Frequency: UNKNOWN; occurs at every drill transfer / transport [ASSUMPTION].

## Product
Hard-shell carry case (60–120 slots) with **screw-top or positive-latch jars**, a case closure that pins jar lids shut, numbered labels, funnel and tray included. Good: lid-retention, uniform jars, case latch, clear lids for colour ID. Bad: snap lids that pop, loose jars rattling, cracked hinges.
