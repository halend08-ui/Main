## Customer
- [INFERENCE] Renters and owners of older/smaller homes whose only "extra" storage is the sink base cabinet, which is broken up by a P-trap, supply lines and often a garbage disposal. They keep cleaning products / toiletries there and can't reach the back.
- [FACT] US homeownership rate was 65.0% in Q2 2026, i.e. ~35% of households rent; under-35 homeownership fell to 35.2% [S-A32]. [INFERENCE] Renters typically cannot install drilled-in cabinet pull-outs, so freestanding organizers are their option.

## Problem
- [FACT] Review roundups report that pipes under the sink "can make it impossible to fit organizers in front or behind them", and that revolving designs fail with center drains/disposals [S-A01].
- [FACT] Wobbling is a recurring complaint: 2-tier units described as "a bit wobbly", drawers wobble when extended with heavy bottles, plastic frames feel flimsy, legs don't lock securely [S-A01].
- [FACT] Drawers that don't slide smoothly / must be lifted to pull out are reported in reviews [S-A33].
- [FACT] Buying guide states under-sink cabinets have vertical drain pipes, disposals that reduce depth and non-standard widths, and frames its guide around "avoid buying something you'll have to return" [S-A07]; an organizing blog reports buying several systems "designed to fit around pipes" that did not work for their configuration [S-A08].
- Frequency: [INFERENCE] daily-access storage, so the pain recurs every time the cabinet is used. Current workarounds: dollar-store bins arranged around pipes, door-mounted racks [S-A08].

## Product
A 2-tier freestanding organizer with **metal frame, ball-bearing or metal-glide pull-out drawers, height-adjustable legs and a width/notch layout that clears a center P-trap** (two narrow units or an L/stepped shape), plus a removable drip tray.
- Good version: steel frame with rust-resistant finish, drawer stops, non-slip feet, clear measuring guide (pipe position templates), ≥2 width options.
- Bad version: thin plastic frame, friction slides, one-size layout that collides with plumbing (the failure modes in [S-A01][S-A33]).

## Shipping concerns
- [ASSUMPTION] Packed weight 5–8 lb and a long box (Walmart listing quotes 34 × 9.6 × 19 in for an expandable rack [S-A02]) → dimensional-weight pricing likely. Non-hazmat, no batteries/liquids.
- Inbound freight per unit is material for a bulky metal item (drives A-LC multiplier).

## Return / breakage concerns
- [INFERENCE] Primary return driver is **fit** (cabinet width/depth/pipe position) — the guide in [S-A07] explicitly frames buying around avoiding returns. High return risk unless sizing is solved pre-purchase.
- Breakage: [INFERENCE] metal frame low risk; plastic clips/slides and bent rails in transit are the likely damage modes.

## Creative angles (genuinely different)
