## Customer
People who travel with several pieces of everyday or fine jewelry (necklaces, studs, hoops, rings) — weekend trips, weddings, business travel — and currently use zip bags or a pouch. Also gift buyers (bridal party, graduation, holiday). [INFERENCE from editorial/gift coverage S-C26, S-C27, S-C36]

## Problem
- Tangled necklaces described as the #1 jewelry-travel complaint by a travel-review site. [FACT as stated by S-C27 — editorial claim, no survey methodology]
- The volume of DIY how-tos (straws, foil, plastic wrap, Ziplocs, toilet-paper rolls, pill organizers) shows people actively seek workarounds. [FACT that these guides exist: S-C33; INFERENCE that this indicates recurring pain]
- Recurring product complaints in reviews: earring holes too large so studs/fish-hooks fall through; no lining on both sides so pieces touch when closed; fabric-sided case collapsed in luggage; too small for hoops/necklace panels; snaps on a roll loosen over time; rolls don't fit large statement necklaces. [FACT per snippets of S-C31, S-C27]

## Product
Compact structured zip case (approx. palm-size) with: individual necklace anchors (snap or clip hangers) + clasp-to-anchor channel, silicone/foam earring board with right-sized holes for studs and hooks, ring roll, lined both sides, rigid shell that doesn't collapse. Good vs bad: anchor count & length, earring retention, crush resistance, zipper. Bad: oversize holes, unlined lid, loose snaps.

## Shipping concerns
Small, light, non-fragile; no batteries/liquids. Rigid shell needs light box to avoid crushing [INFERENCE].

## Return / breakage concerns
Low breakage. Returns: colour/aesthetic, "smaller than expected" (size complaints in S-C31) → publish exact interior dimensions and capacity photos.

## Creative angles (genuinely different)
1. "Shake test" — necklaces still untangled after shaking the closed case.
2. Stud-earring retention close-up vs oversize-hole boards.
3. Wedding/bridal-party gift set (monogram later).
4. Carry-on minimalism: what fits for a 7-day trip.
5. Theft/loss angle: keeping valuables together in a hotel safe.

## Differentiation opportunity
Engineering the specific complaint list (right-size earring holes, two-sided lining, crush-proof shell, necklace anchors that fit long chains) at $30–$40, with honest capacity specs. [INFERENCE] Differentiation is narrow — the leaders already market "tangle-free"; aesthetics and brand matter heavily.
