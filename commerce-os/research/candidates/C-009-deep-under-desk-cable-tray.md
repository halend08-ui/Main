## Customer
Home workers with a desk against a wall or in a shared room — especially sit-stand desk and glass-top desk owners who cannot or will not drill — whose power strip, laptop/monitor power bricks and cables hang or pile on the floor. [INFERENCE from product positioning in S-C01, S-C04 and "no drill / standing desk / glass desk" keywords in listing titles]

## Problem
- Cable clutter under the desk; floor-level power strips; cables snagging when a standing desk moves. [INFERENCE from listing positioning S-C01, S-C04]
- **Existing trays are too shallow/narrow for power bricks:** a review on an Amazon no-drill tray listing reportedly says the tray "fell short in height and width" so it was very hard to fit 2 power bricks plus cables on a glass-top standing desk; the same reviewer had trouble removing a red sticker and its glue. [FACT as shown in search snippet of S-C01; page not opened]
- A review site reports trays under ~40 mm deep failed its tests and depth under ~35 mm won't fit a typical laptop power brick. [FACT as reported by S-C02, weak source — methodology not verified]
- **Clamp fit limits:** clamps typically fit desk edges ~0.4–1.6 in thick; rounded/beveled edges may not grip; some arms bend when over-tightened; "the desk is fine, but the tray is wobbly." [FACT per snippets of S-C08, S-C02]
- Workarounds: drill-mounted trays, IKEA-type cheap trays, zip ties, adhesive clips, leaving it on the floor. [INFERENCE]
- Frequency: UNKNOWN. Daily visible annoyance for affected users [ASSUMPTION].

## Product
A powder-coated steel (or aluminium) under-desk tray, no-drill C-clamp mount, with:
- ≥45–50 mm internal depth and width that fits a 6-outlet strip plus 2 laptop bricks (spec to be confirmed against measured bricks).
- Wide clamp range incl. thin and thick tops; rubber pads to protect desk/glass; optional screw holes.
- Rigid arms that do not bend at specified torque; no residue stickers.
Good vs bad: depth, clamp range, arm rigidity, finish (no sharp mesh edges), clear compatibility chart. Bad: shallow 35 mm trays, flimsy arms, adhesive stickers.
**The product is passive metal — it carries no current.** Marketing must not imply any electrical/fire-safety rating.

## Shipping concerns
Long, flat steel part → awkward carton, dimensional-weight pricing likely [INFERENCE]. Non-fragile, no batteries/liquids. Weight UNKNOWN until sample.

## Return / breakage concerns
Fit returns: desk too thick/thin, beveled edge, under-desk frame crossbar collides with clamp [INFERENCE from S-C08]. Breakage low (steel); finish scratches in transit possible. Bent arms if over-tightened [S-C02].

## Creative angles (genuinely different)
1. "Fits two laptop bricks" — side-by-side brick test vs a shallow tray.
2. Standing-desk motion test: raise/lower with nothing dragging on the floor.
3. Renter/glass-desk angle: zero holes, padded clamps.
4. Robot-vacuum / pet angle: floor cleared of cords.
5. 3-minute install timelapse.
