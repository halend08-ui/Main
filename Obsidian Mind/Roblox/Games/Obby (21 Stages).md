# Obby (21 Stages)

Back to [[Roblox Home]] · Grew into [[Grow an Obby]]

My first build: a basic obby with 21 checkpointed stages.

- **Structure:** course parts live in `Workspace > Course`, checkpoints in `Workspace > Checkpoints` (named `1`, `2`, `3`...).
- **Parts:** `Kill`, `Spinner` and `Fader` (the naming convention that carried over into Grow an Obby).
- **To add a stage:** copy the last checkpoint, rename it to the next number, then build the course leading up to it.
- **Monetization:** Skip Stage (Developer Product, R$ 15–25) and Speed Boost (Pass, R$ 49–99). IDs go in `ReplicatedStorage > ObbyConfig`.
- **Name idea:** "Easy Obby 🌈 21 Stages"

## What it taught me
- A config module plus parts named by what they do makes a game easy to extend.
- Icons and thumbnails matter more than almost anything else for getting clicks.
- To grow: post Shorts of the hardest stages, then run small ads once the game is earning.
