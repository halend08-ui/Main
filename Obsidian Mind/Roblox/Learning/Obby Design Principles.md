# Obby design principles

Back to [[Roblox Home]] · Used in [[Grow an Obby]]

## Difficulty curve
- **Teach, test, twist.** New mechanic, safe version → harder version → combined with an older mechanic.
- Ramp in a sawtooth: each zone starts a little easier than the last zone ended, then climbs.
- Place checkpoints often. Losing more than 30 seconds of progress makes players quit.
- End each zone with a "boss" level, then a pop-up reward moment.

## One new idea per level type
Each level type should have **one** readable rule, which the player can see without reading text:
- Colors mean something: red = kills, green = safe, yellow = bounce.
- Hazards move in a steady rhythm so players can learn them. Random timing feels unfair.
- Telegraph danger: Faders flash before they disappear, spinners have a visible arm.

## Level-type toolkit (current + ideas)
Current: Kill, Spinner, Fader, Grower, Bounce, poison swamp, thorn spinner, wilting leaf, growing sprout, cactus field, beanstalk climb, *+12 new in v8 (list in [[Grow an Obby]])*.

Ideas for later:
- Conveyor vines (push you sideways)
- Wind gusts (pushes the player in bursts)
- Seed cannons (launch pads with a set path)
- Water that rises over time (race to the top)
- Day/night flowers: platforms that exist only in one lighting phase
- Sticky sap (slows you down) / slippery dew (low friction)
- A moving truss ladder climb

## Feel
- Sound + particles on checkpoint, death and zone complete.
- Death should be fast: respawn in about 1 second, with no long animation.
- Mobile: wider jumps than on PC and bigger platforms early on. Test on a phone.
