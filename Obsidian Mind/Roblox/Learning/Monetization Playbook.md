# Monetization playbook

Back to [[Roblox Home]] · Used in [[Grow an Obby#Monetization]]

## The money types
| Type | Bought | Good for | In my games |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Developer Product** | Again and again | Skips, revives, currency, boosts | Skip Level R$ 25 |
| **Pass** | Once, owned forever | Perks, VIP, cosmetics | Speed Boost 99, Super Jump 149, Rainbow Trail 49 |
| **Premium Payouts** | Automatic | Paid by how long Premium players play | Free money from retention |
| **Private servers** | Subscription | Friends playing together | Consider enabling (cheap, e.g. R$ 50–100/mo) |
| **Avatar items / UGC** | n/a | Bigger games with a brand | Later |

## What works in obbies
- **Skip** is the best earner. Offer it at the *moment of frustration*: after 3–5 deaths on the same level, show a gentle "Skip this level?" prompt.
- **Cheap first purchase.** A R$ 25–49 item gets players to buy once, and people who have bought once buy again more easily.
- **Cosmetics** (trails, pets, hats) sell steadily and don't make the game unfair.
- **VIP / Starter bundle** as a mid-priced "best value" option.
- Show what's for sale **in the world** too (a VIP door, a trail you can see on the pedestal), not only in the shop.

## Rules
- Don't block progress behind a paywall. Every level must be beatable for free, or the reviews and retention will suffer.
- Every item is free in Studio while its ID is `0`, which is fine for testing. **Double-check the IDs before publishing.**
- Change one price at a time and wait a week before comparing.
- Numbers to watch: **conversion rate** (the share of players who pay), **ARPPU** (average spend per paying player) and **revenue per visit**.

## Money out
- DevEx converts Robux to cash once you meet the requirements (a minimum balance and a verified account in good standing). Check the current rules on create.roblox.com.
- Roblox takes a cut of each sale, so plan on receiving about 70% of a Pass or Developer Product price.
